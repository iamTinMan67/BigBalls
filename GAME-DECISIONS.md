# Game decisions captured for prototype

## Confirmed direction
- Two remote players and one Game Master.
- Three rounds: Quick-fire: Build Your Arsenal; Grab My Balls; The Big Balls Showdown.
- Game Master operates the real pool table and records outcomes.
- One host miss allowance per player per round.
- Final showdown: four object balls per player plus the black and cue ball.
- Bank Time and Boycie Bucks carry between rounds.
- Question formats include trivia, quick thinking, estimation, visual, entertainment, true/false, multiple choice, free answer, higher/lower and riddles.
- Steals are only available on selected question types.
- Adults-only cheeky British pub humour, not excessively smutty or rude.
- Host is Game Master, not required to be a showman.
- Built-in broadcast/voice communication is the eventual direction; no recording in the initial version.
- No permanent cloud game database is required; session results should be exportable locally.

## Open decisions — not silently finalised
- Ball group allocation.
- Bank Time exhaustion.
- Detailed foul consequences and repeat-miss penalties.
- Premature black-ball consequence.
- Exact Bank Time / BB$ rewards, costs and penalties.
- Power Locker supply, ownership, single-use rules and auction.
- Prediction and Bingo rules/rewards.
- Steal rewards and exact eligible question formats.
- Final technical choice for remote signalling, voice/video and broadcast infrastructure.

## Prototype-only provisional values
The first local prototype uses adjustable test values to make the flow playable: a correct answer awards 5 seconds and 25 BB$; a pot awards 10 seconds and 50 BB$; a foul currently subtracts 5 seconds. These are not final game rules.
